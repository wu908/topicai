# Root Publication Work in Content Intent

TopicAI uses one Primary Content Intent as the root of each single-publication
Content Project. A Publish Judgment shares an audience, response, evidence,
uncertainty, and observation spine while requiring intent-specific claims;
intent remains correctable until Intent Lock, after which the Publication
Intent is immutable and later classification is appended. Creator Series are
linked by an ongoing audience promise and may contain different intents and
formats, rejecting the previous universal problem-answer form and same-intent
series constraint.
