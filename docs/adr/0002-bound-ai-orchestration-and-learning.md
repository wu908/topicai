# Bound AI Orchestration and Learning

TopicAI presents one action prepared by an AI Orchestrator while a deterministic
Action Policy owns eligibility, safety vetoes, and Human Gates. AI work remains
bounded by source-traceable Evidence; platform metrics do not prove success,
relative ranges require at least three window-matched Comparable Samples, and
long-term learning requires repeated reviewed outcomes plus user confirmation.
Automatic preparation is authorized per capability after three accepted
results, never by a global trust score, and never includes protected decisions.
